# Legal Hold Guide

## Trigger
- Litigation anticipated.
- Regulatory investigation.
- Subpoena.

## Immediate Actions
1. Legal issues hold notice.
2. IT suspends deletion.
3. Custodians acknowledge.
4. Data preserved with chain of custody.

## Release
- Written release from legal.
- Resume retention.
- Secure disposal.

## Forbidden
- Deletion after hold (spoliation).
- Access by non-legal personnel.
- Automatic release.
