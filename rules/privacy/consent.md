# Consent Management

## Requirements
- Freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous.
- Granular: separate consent per purpose.
- Withdrawable: as easy as giving.
- Recorded: timestamp, version of the privacy notice, scope.

## UI
- No pre-checked boxes.
- No dark patterns (bundled consent, hidden reject).
- Reject as easy as accept.
- Consent wall only where legally permitted.

## Storage
- Consent receipts stored immutably.
- Linked to user identity.
- Auditable: who, when, what, version.

## Propagation
- Consent state propagated to all processing systems.
- Withdrawal triggers deletion or restriction within SLA (30 days).
- Third parties notified of withdrawal.

## Forbidden
- Bundled consent across purposes.
- Silent renewal.
- Consent for children without verifiable parental consent.
- Dark patterns in the consent UI.
