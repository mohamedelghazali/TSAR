# Incident Communications

## Internal
- Executive notification within 30 minutes of declaration.
- Status updates every 30-60 minutes.
- Single source of truth (status page or incident channel).
- Roles: Incident Commander, Communications Lead, Scribe.

## External
- Customer notification within regulatory timeline.
- Transparency: what happened, impact, actions, next update.
- Legal review before external statements.
- No speculation about cause or attribution.

## Regulatory
| Regulation | Timeline |
|---|---|
| GDPR | 72 hours to supervisory authority |
| HIPAA | 60 days to individuals, HHS |
| PCI DSS | 24 hours to acquirer |
| SEC (US public companies) | 4 business days (Form 8-K) |
| NIS2 | 24 hours initial, 72 hours detailed |

## Channels
- Email, status page, social media (if required).
- Customer support trained on talking points.
- Legal and PR aligned.

## Post-Incident
- Post-mortem within 2 weeks.
- Public post-mortem for significant incidents.
- Lessons learned tracked to closure.

## Forbidden
- Delaying notification without legal basis.
- Statements without legal review.
- Blaming attackers or individuals publicly.
