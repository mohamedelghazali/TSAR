# Legal Hold

## When to Apply
- Litigation is reasonably anticipated.
- Regulatory investigation.
- Subpoena or court order.
- Internal investigation with legal exposure.

## Preservation Scope
- Custodians: identify individuals with relevant data.
- Systems: email, chat, files, databases, logs, mobile devices.
- Time range: covered by the matter.
- Suspend automated deletion in scope.

## Process
1. Legal issues hold notice to custodians.
2. IT suspends deletion in identified systems.
3. Custodians acknowledge in writing.
4. Data preserved in read-only, forensically sound form.
5. Chain of custody documented.

## Storage
- Encrypted at rest.
- Access restricted to legal and forensics.
- Integrity hash recorded.
- Retention until legal releases the hold.

## Release
- Written release from legal.
- Resume normal retention.
- Secure disposal per rules/data/secure-deletion.md.

## Forbidden
- Deletion after hold notice (spoliation).
- Notifying custodians to delete.
- Access by non-legal personnel.
- Automatic release without legal approval.
