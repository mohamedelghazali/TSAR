# Privilege Protection

## Attorney-Client Privilege
- Communications between legal and client for legal advice.
- Mark documents "Privileged & Confidential — Attorney Work Product".
- Limit distribution to those with need to know.

## Work Product
- Materials prepared in anticipation of litigation.
- Protected from disclosure in many jurisdictions.

## Handling
- Store privileged materials in separate repositories.
- Access restricted to legal + named advisors.
- Never forward to third parties without legal approval.
- Redact privileged content from general reports.

## Waiver Risk
- Sharing privilege can waive it.
- External auditors, consultants, regulators may need agreements.
- Review before any external sharing.

## Forbidden
- Mixing privileged with non-privileged in the same file.
- Forwarding privileged content to distribution lists.
- Discussing privileged matters in unencrypted channels.
