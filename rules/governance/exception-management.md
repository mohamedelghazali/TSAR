# Security Exception Management

## When to Request
- Business requirement conflicts with a security rule.
- Technical constraint prevents immediate compliance.
- Third-party dependency limitation.

## Process
1. Document the exception: rule, justification, risk, compensating controls.
2. Risk acceptance signed by the data/application owner.
3. Security team review and approval.
4. Time-bound: maximum 90 days, renewable with re-review.
5. Tracked in a central register.

## Compensating Controls
- Additional monitoring.
- Restricted access.
- Network segmentation.
- Reduced functionality.

## Review
- Weekly review of active exceptions.
- Monthly report to security leadership.
- Quarterly re-evaluation of all exceptions.

## Forbidden
- Permanent exceptions.
- Exceptions without owner sign-off.
- Exceptions not tracked in the register.
- "Temporary" exceptions older than 90 days without renewal.
