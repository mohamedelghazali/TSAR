# Risk Assessment

## Methodology
- Asset identification.
- Threat modeling (STRIDE, MITRE ATT&CK).
- Vulnerability identification.
- Likelihood and impact scoring (NIST 800-30 or FAIR).
- Risk rating: Critical, High, Medium, Low.

## Cadence
- Annual full assessment.
- Per-feature for Tier-1 changes.
- Post-incident reassessment.
- Continuous monitoring for new threats.

## Risk Register
- Every risk tracked with:
  - ID, title, description.
  - Owner.
  - Likelihood, impact, rating.
  - Mitigation plan, due date.
  - Residual risk after mitigation.
- Reviewed monthly by security leadership.

## Acceptance
- Low: owner sign-off.
- Medium: security team sign-off.
- High: CISO sign-off.
- Critical: business disruption until mitigated.

## Forbidden
- Risks without owners.
- Risks not tracked in the register.
- Critical risks without immediate mitigation plan.
