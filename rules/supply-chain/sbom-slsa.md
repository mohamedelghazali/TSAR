# SBOM and SLSA

## SBOM
- Format: CycloneDX or SPDX.
- Generated at build time; attached as an attestation to the image/artifact.
- Includes all direct and transitive dependencies with versions and hashes.
- Machine-readable; queryable by consumers.
- Retained for the life of the artifact plus the retention period.

## SLSA Level
- Minimum SLSA Build Level 2 for all production artifacts.
- Level 3 required for Tier-1 artifacts (auth, crypto, payments).
- Build platform: isolated, ephemeral, with provenance generated by the platform.

## Provenance
- Attach provenance to every artifact (in-toto, Sigstore).
- Provenance includes: source commit, build config, builder identity, dependencies.
- Verified at admission (Kubernetes, deployment pipelines).

## Verification
- Consumers verify signatures and provenance before deployment.
- Reject artifacts without valid provenance in strict mode.

## Forbidden
- Building artifacts on developer laptops for production.
- Unsigned artifacts in production.
- SBOM generation as a post-hoc step (must be at build).
