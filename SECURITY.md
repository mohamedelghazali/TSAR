# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability
Email the maintainers directly. Do not disclose publicly until a fix is available.

## Scope
- Rules that permit unsafe defaults.
- Missing coverage for a known CWE or OWASP category.
- Scanners that miss a well-known pattern.

## Response
- Acknowledgment within 72 hours.
- Fix or mitigation within 30 days for high-severity issues.
- Coordinated disclosure timeline agreed with the reporter.
